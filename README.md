## Research direction

Most artificial-intelligence systems begin with prediction. Our public research asks a different question: what changes when memory, coherence, orientation, and accountable action are treated as first-class computational primitives?

These repositories are runnable research artifacts—not claims of production-ready AGI. They document an evolving experimental program and invite inspection, testing, criticism, and extension.

## Principles

- **Remembrance before prediction** — persistent context is treated as structural.
- **Coherence before expansion** — systems should expose instability.
- **Human agency** — tools should support reflection and accountable choice.
- **Inspectable prototypes** — public artifacts should be runnable and clearly scoped.
- **Responsible claims** — simulations and symbolic models are identified as such.
